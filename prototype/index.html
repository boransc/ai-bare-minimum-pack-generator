The purpose

AI adoption often runs ahead of the controls around it.

The AI Bare Minimum Pack is designed for that reality. It helps an organisation establish the minimum governance around existing or emerging AI use, then close the most important gaps over thirty days.

It is not a new policy written by an algorithm. The source standard, guidance, policy template and staff note remain authoritative; the prototype only contextualises how that material is presented.

Full AI Playbook

From the minimum standard to a board-ready governance system.

The Bare Minimum Pack closes immediate gaps. The Full AI Playbook is a deeper organisation-specific review built from evidence, with seven connected parts.

01

Where the organisation stands

Your evidence base, current position, risk picture and review against the AI Governance Code.

02

AI strategy

Vision, commitments, strategic alignment, use cases, risk appetite, resources and roadmap.

03

Governance architecture

Charter, decision rights, governance body, the Three Lines and board oversight.

04

Policy framework

The current policy estate, priority fixes, supporting standards and an AI-impact sweep.

05

Transparency

Proportionate disclosure, the AI Transparency Index, human review and external reporting.

06

Assurance

A ten-domain audit universe, assurance ratings, independence and a multi-year plan.

07

Implementation

A ninety-day sprint, twelve-month programme, training, dependencies and review triggers.

This prototype previews the pathway only. A Full AI Playbook would be created from the organisation’s evidence; no client example content is reused.
