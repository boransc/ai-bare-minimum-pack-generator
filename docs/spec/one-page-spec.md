# AI Bare Minimum Pack Generator — one-page specification

**Governance AI · Draft for client review · 1 September 2026**

## What it is

A short, free-to-complete web product. An organisation answers eight context questions, then completes
Governance AI's existing eight-point **AI Minimum Standard**. It receives immediately:

- a clear verdict on whether the minimum is met, with its score out of eight
- the specific sub-points it does not yet evidence
- a tailored version of **The AI Bare Minimum Pack** — the eight-point check, the starter guidance note,
  the AI usage policy template and the staff note
- a checklist it can return to, following Karl's own thirty-day plan
- a printable / downloadable pack, with the policy and staff note as editable documents
- a personal return link
- a clear pathway into Governance AI's full governance of AI review

## The governing rule

**The Pack is authoritative and does not change.** Structure, the eight controls, the sub-statements,
the scoring, the bands, the red lines, the policy template and the staff note are fixed source content,
stored as versioned files and rendered exactly as written.

AI is used only to **contextualise**: emphasis, language, sector-appropriate examples, realistic risk
scenarios and short organisation-specific explanations. It fills a fixed set of named, length-capped
fields assembled *alongside* the source text. It cannot rewrite a control, a policy clause, a score or a
next step, because it is never given the ability to emit one.

**It never invents policy, regulation, sector requirements or organisational facts.** That is enforced
structurally — narrow inputs, a fixed schema, automated checks, and a plain deterministic fallback if
anything fails, so the pack always renders. Where a rule is genuinely outside the bare minimum, the
product says so in the source's own words and points to the fuller review.

## The eight context questions

Sector · size · how AI is used today · what it is used for · whether it touches confidential, client or
personal information · whether the organisation is regulated · whether AI plays a part in decisions about
people · whether one named person at board or senior level is accountable.

Fixed options only, no free text, and nothing we would not put on a postcard. Organisation name is
optional. "Don't know" is an honest, available answer throughout and becomes a *find out* action; it never
counts as a pass.

## How the assessment behaves

Presented as the eight controls, answered at the level of the source's own sub-statements. A point scores
**Yes only when every applicable sub-statement is evidenced** — no partial credit, exactly as the Standard
requires. **Points 4 and 6 are red lines:** a no against either means the minimum is not met whatever the
total says, and the result page leads with that.

The completed assessment is a **dated snapshot** — the signed copy the Standard asks for. Working through
the checklist does not quietly raise the score; to move the score the organisation re-takes the assessment,
which produces a new dated snapshot and leaves the old one intact.

## Privacy and data

No account and no login. The return link is the only way back: an unguessable token, not indexed by search
engines, expiring after 90 days, with saved packs deleted after 12 months. Email is optional, used only to
send the return link, and contains the link alone — never the score or the gaps. Marketing consent is a
separate, unticked opt-in. Governance AI can see completions and scores as qualified leads, and this is
stated plainly to the user at the point of capture rather than implied away.

## The Full Playbook pathway

Presented as the natural next step for every organisation, in the source's own account of what the bare
minimum does *not* give you — risk appetite, board capability, use-case assessment, regulatory readiness,
vendor due diligence, assurance. The recommendation strengthens where the answers show a specific gap: no
board-level owner, AI touching consequential decisions about people, or no AI policy at all.

## Versioning

Every pack is pinned to the source version that produced it and is never silently changed. If a newer
version of the Standard exists, the organisation is offered the option to regenerate, which creates a new
pack and preserves the original.

## Before launch, Governance AI needs to confirm

1. **Content sign-off** — that the structured, machine-readable Pack faithfully represents the original.
2. **Brand and trademark sign-off** — no brand kit has been supplied, so the current visual treatment is
   provisional. Supplied names and trademarks are preserved exactly as written in the source.
3. **Privacy notice wording** — covering lead visibility, the 90-day and 12-month lifetimes, and consent.
4. **One open question:** should returning to a saved pack extend the 90 days, or does the link expire 90
   days after creation regardless? The build assumes the latter.
